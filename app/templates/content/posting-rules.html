What belongs here

Product quality, colour, pattern, sizing, fabric, packaging, delivery, value, and questions that help someone understand the item are welcome. Positive and negative opinions are treated the same.

What is not allowed

Do not post links, repeated messages, harassment, obscenity, sexual content, private information, advertising, or content unrelated to the product. Review photos, once enabled, must be appropriate for a general audience.

How moderation works

Automated checks remove prohibited content and collapse posts that may be off topic. A collapsed post remains available behind a notice. Store staff can correct a false positive, hide or restore a post, and reply publicly as GG Afrikan Eleganz.

Questions or corrections

If you believe a post was handled incorrectly, use the Contact page. See the Privacy Policy for information about moderation records.
